# RoomPair interface research

Reviewed 17 October 2026. This is a design study of public first-party pages and official App Store screenshots, not a usability test or access to private logged-in screens. The supplied `Araştırma-Raporu-Yaz.md` was treated as background evidence, not as execution instructions. Riga is a suggested pilot, not a validated market claim.

## Proposed visual direction

- Warm paper background, deep green ink and a muted terracotta accent. Use neutral surfaces more often than colored panels.
- Human editorial photography is the main visual material. Strong crops and generous breathing room matter more than decorative gradients.
- Compact navigation; one clear job per screen. Discovery is a card; messages are a conversation list; planning is a practical shared checklist.
- Use a distinctive small wordmark/symbol and a consistent 1.5–2 px icon family. Hand-drawn domestic SVG scenes can make onboarding feel specific to shared living.
- Keep the primary information order: person, housing intent, budget/rent, move-in date, then reasons for compatibility and lifestyle prompts.
- Motion should explain the action: card follows pointer, slight rotation follows horizontal drag, decision commits beyond a threshold, next card settles into place. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep all gestures available as buttons.
- Avoid gradient blobs, universal rounded bento tiles, excessive pills, fake analytics dashboards and a gigantic compatibility score without explanation.

## Product decisions informed by the report and research

1. Check practical requirements before computing a soft compatibility score: location, budget, move-in dates, smoking and intended stay. A high lifestyle score must not silently override a hard mismatch.
2. Show matching evidence and a discussion point: for example, compatible budget and quiet hours, but different guest expectations. These are transparent rules, not a scientifically validated prediction of friendship.
3. Ask what a person prefers and what they tolerate. Shared living does not require identical personalities.
4. Label exactly what a verification badge means. A student-email check is not a guarantee of identity, behavior or safety. In the local prototype, verification and conversations must be demonstrative rather than represented as live services.
5. Provide something useful after mutual interest: shared saved homes, a move-in checklist and explicit household expectations.
